Problem

HR teams need a shared path from an AI idea to a scoped workflow, accountable owners, and a reasoned pilot decision.

Approach

Resolve: HR Workflow and Judgment

Follow a fictional HR request from policy lookup to a draft response and simulated review. Inspect the boundary between routine support and a concern that needs specialist judgment.

Read case study +

Challenge

A correctly identified HR topic can still hide a concern requiring specialist handling. The design question is where automation should stop and a person should take ownership.

Approach

Defined the workflow and evaluation questions, then built the prototype with AI-assisted development. The browser demo uses rules and fixed-template responses. Review is simulated, activity history is temporary, and no employment action is executed. An optional model screen is a separate experiment.

Outcome

The original rules passed 60 development cases but met none of the complete pass conditions in 16 developer-authored paraphrases. Missed specialist concerns showed why topic matching alone was insufficient. The preserved evaluation explains those failures and their effect on the design; it does not establish independent validation.

Project scope: reference tools, proposed pilots, and synthetic demonstrations. No measured business outcomes or production readiness are claimed. Resolve simulates review and remains unsuitable for real HR use. Its evaluation evidence and limits are available with the source.
